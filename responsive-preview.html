Summary

Policy: https://example.com/privacy-policy

Example.com is a service provider that collects account details you give it directly — name, email address and password — alongside data generated as you use the site, such as pages viewed, search queries and the device and browser you connect from. It also states that it receives limited information from advertising and analytics partners.

That information is used to run and improve the service, personalize content, measure performance, prevent fraud and send marketing where you have opted in. The policy says data may be shared with service providers who process it on the company's behalf, with advertising and analytics partners, and with authorities where legally required; it does not claim to sell personal information outright, though the wording around "partners" is broad.

Retention periods are described by category rather than as fixed terms, and account data is kept until you delete your account. You can access, correct, export or delete your data from account settings, opt out of marketing at any time, and exercise region-specific rights such as objection or restriction by contacting the privacy team. Cookies include essential, analytics and advertising types, with a consent banner offered in some regions, and the policy notes that not all trackers are described in detail.
